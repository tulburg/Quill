## Purpose
The menu bar panel is the exact Quill main window (sidebar, Home, editor, everything as Quiet.md draws it), with one addition: a "New note" field at the top of Home. Focusing it expands it into a new entry while the Home content slides out the bottom, so capturing a note is one click away. No custom UI for the panel.

## Requirements
1. The panel shows the same Workspace view as the main window, unchanged. The only new element is a single-line "New note" field at the top of the Home content, under the Home header (title, search, actions) and above the My documents / Favorites tabs.
2. Focusing the field starts the transition: the field grows into the new note's editor and the Home content below it slides down and out of the bottom edge, fading. The sidebar and header stay put.
3. The transition is smooth (no dropped frames at 120 Hz): 240 ms ease-out, reversed on Esc; the timings live in theme tokens.
4. When it finishes, the new note is open in the normal editor exactly as Quiet.md shows any note (title "Untitled", typed text as the first body line per D-9, listed in the sidebar).
5. A document is created on disk only once it has content; leaving an empty entry creates nothing.
6. Esc from an empty new entry reverses the transition back to Home. A non-empty note is saved and stays in the library like any other note.
7. Reduce Motion: a 120 ms cross-fade instead of the slide.
8. The field appears in the menu bar panel only (D-8), not in the standalone window.

## Out of scope
The status item and the panel itself (S-7). Any other change to the main window.

## Design
Three frames on this card: resting, mid-transition, expanded.

## Acceptance checks
- Open panel on Home: the main window as is, with the field on top of Home.
- Click the field: it grows into the editor, Home content slides out the bottom, focus is in the body.
- Esc on an empty entry: back to Home, no new document in the library.
- Type, then go Home: the note is listed and saved on disk.
- Reduce Motion on: cross-fade, no slide.
- The standalone window's Home has no field.
