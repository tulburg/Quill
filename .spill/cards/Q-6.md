Quiet.md uses Option+Space for its quick note window. In Quill the panel and the new entry field cover that job.

- A. Option+Space toggles the panel and focuses the new entry field; the separate quick note window goes away (recommended)
- B. Option+Space keeps the quick note window; a new shortcut toggles the panel
- C. No default shortcut, user sets one
