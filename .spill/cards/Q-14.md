S-6 needs a Quill app icon and a menu bar template icon (req 4). Three directions are drawn on the S-6 design board, each shown as the app icon and as the 18pt menu bar icon on light and dark bars.

- A. Feather: a white feather on a dark graphite tile; the menu bar icon is the same feather.
- B. Page and quill: a note page with a small feather on a warm paper tile; the menu bar icon is the page and feather.
- C. Q with a feather tail: a white Q on blue whose tail is a feather; at 18pt it can read as a search icon.
