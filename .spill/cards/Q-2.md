The 67 icons in `assets/icons/` are under Quiet.md's commercial Nucleo license and cannot be reused in a fork.

- A. Swap to an open icon set (Lucide, ISC license) matched to the same sizes (recommended)
- B. Buy our own Nucleo license and keep the same set
- C. Draw our own
