Quiet.md stores its library in `~/Documents/Quiet.md`. Quill can use its own folder or open the same library so notes show in both apps (both apps writing the same files is supported upstream for multiple windows, not for two apps).

- A. Own library at `~/Documents/Quill` (recommended)
- B. Share `~/Documents/Quiet.md`
- C. Own library, with a one-time "Import from Quiet.md" on first launch
