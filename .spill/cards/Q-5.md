The panel holds the full app (home, tree, editor), so size matters.

- A. Attached popover under the icon with an arrow, fixed size, closes when you click elsewhere
- B. Borderless panel under the icon, resizable (size remembered), closes when you click elsewhere (recommended)
- C. Same as B, but stays open until dismissed (can be pinned)
